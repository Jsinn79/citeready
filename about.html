I started CiteReady after noticing the same thing over and over: I'd ask an AI assistant for a local business, and the answer would name two or three places — never the same ones a Google search would have shown me, and often not the best clinic in town. Just the most quotable one.

That gap is invisible from the inside. A clinic owner can be paying for SEO, posting daily, holding a 4.9 rating, and still be entirely absent from the answer a patient gets when they ask their phone who to trust. Nobody sends you a report about it, because it isn't in anyone's report yet.

So I built one. Twelve real customer questions, five AI assistants, screenshots of every answer, and a ranked list of what to change. I run each audit by hand — no scraper, no white-label vendor, no dashboard login to forget about.
